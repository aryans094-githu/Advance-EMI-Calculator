# 6. REDUCING-BALANCE EMI CALCULATION

For a standard monthly reducing-balance loan:

P = financed principal

r = periodic interest rate

n = total number of installments

EMI:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n - 1)

If annual interest rate is R:

r = R / 12 / 100

For other frequencies, calculate the periodic rate appropriately and clearly document the assumption.

For every reducing-balance calculation show:

* EMI
* Total number of payments
* Total interest
* Total repayment
* Principal repaid
* Interest paid

---

# 7. FLAT-RATE LOAN CALCULATION

Support flat-rate loans separately.

For annual flat rate R:

Total Interest:

Interest = Principal × R/100 × Tenure in Years

Total Repayment:

Total Repayment = Principal + Total Interest

For monthly installments:

EMI = Total Repayment / Number of Installments

Example:

Principal = ₹5,00,000

Flat interest rate = 10%

Tenure = 5 years

Interest:

₹5,00,000 × 10% × 5

= ₹2,50,000

Total repayment:

₹7,50,000

Monthly EMI:

₹7,50,000 / 60

= ₹12,500

Make it very clear that a flat rate and reducing-balance rate are **not directly comparable by simply looking at the stated percentage**.

# 33. SELF-CHECKING LOOP

For every financial calculation:

STEP 1:
Calculate using the primary formula.

STEP 2:
Calculate using an independent method.

STEP 3:
Compare the results.

STEP 4:
If the difference exceeds the defined tolerance, investigate.

STEP 5:
Check edge cases.

STEP 6:
Check rounding.

STEP 7:
Check the amortization schedule.

STEP 8:
Confirm:

Sum of principal components ≈ financed principal

Sum of interest components ≈ total interest

Sum of payments ≈ total repayment

Final balance ≈ zero

STEP 9:
Only then display the result.

Never silently accept a calculation that fails validation.
